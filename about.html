Experience

Laboratory Assistant

May 2026 – Aug 2026 Institut Lafayette · Metz, France

At Institut Lafayette's Class 100 cleanroom, I had the unique opportunity to work on the microfabrication of gas sensors. We took the wafers and inserted them into a PECVD chamber where a layer of Silicon Nitride was deposited on to a GaN layer. We then defined active regions using photolithography by applying spin-coating photoresist on top of the wafers, and exposing the wafers to UV light. I then got to utilize a dry plasma tool to etch the wafers and dipped them into a solvent bath. Finally, we did ellipsometry checks to monitor the thickness of the wafers throughout the entire process.

Undergraduate Researcher

May 2026 – Aug 2026 Georgia Institute of Technology Research · Metz, France

At Georgia Tech Europe, I got involved in undergraduate research concerning Micro-LED cochlear implants. Our project built on a simluation in COMSOL Multiphyics that modeled heat propogation of these cochlear implants. I first familiarized myself with 1-2D finite-difference time-domain simulations in python. We then built on previous literature by aiming to flip the LED duty cycles and extending the duration to analyze the thermal behavior in the long-run.
