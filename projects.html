Ceiling Light System Completed

Georgia Tech · Aug 2025 – Mar 2026

During my first semester at Georgia Tech, I developed a ESP32-controlled lighting system along with four other people. We used fuse blocks and buck converters to step down voltages (24V power supply to 5V for LED lanes and hardware), and the circuit itself contained logic level shifters to account for the ESP32 (3.3V) and LED lanes (5V). The biggest learning curve from this project was a signal reflection issue that occurred with 8 lanes. We ultimately routed each lane through a buffered output on the level shifter and opted for the installation of just 6 lanes.
